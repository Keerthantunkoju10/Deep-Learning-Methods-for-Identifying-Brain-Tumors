🧠 Neural System Topology

Dual-Stage Deep Learning Pipeline

How high-resolution MRI scans are processed through our multi-tiered convolutional architecture.

01

Otsu Preprocessing

Scans are converted to normalized grayscale, scaled to standardized tensor dimensions (128x128 and 64x64), and normalized to range [-1.0, 1.0].

OpenCV + NumPy
02

CNN Classifier Backbone

Dual Convolutional 2D layers (32 filters, 3x3 kernels) with MaxPooling (2x2), Flatten, and Dense Softmax outputs predict tumor presence.

Sequential CNN
03

U-Net Segmentation

Deep encoder-decoder architecture with 4 skip connections and transposed convolutions that maps pixel-level spatial probabilities for the tumor body.

19-Layer U-Net
04

Morphometric Contours

Extracts perimeter polygons and bounding boxes around detected tumors, filtering artifact noise to deliver actionable surgical boundaries.

Contour Analysis

💎 Core Capabilities

Engineered for Accuracy & Explainability

🔍

Dual-Stage Detection

Instead of relying on a single black-box classification, the pipeline isolates tumor presence first, then applies spatial U-Net segmentation.

📐

Morphological Perimeter Tracing

Calculates precise perimeter contours and bounding boxes, providing quantitative tumor pixel surface area estimation.

🌈

Thermal False-Color Heatmaps

Applies a JET colormap overlay on the MRI tissue gradient to enhance visual interpretability for clinical review.

⚡

Sub-Second Latency

Optimized tensor operations deliver predictions in under 100 milliseconds, suitable for point-of-care rapid screening.

🛡️

Robust Error Handling

Safely handles various image orientations, formats, noise artifacts, and multi-channel medical scan inputs.

💻

Cross-Platform Deployment

Run locally as a modern responsive web app or as a native desktop application via the bundled launcher.
